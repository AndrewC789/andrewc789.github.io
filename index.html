Hi! I'm Andrew Clickard

I'm a fifth year Mathematics PhD candidate at Temple University working with Dave Futer. I'm interested in knot theory and low-dimensional geometric topology, and more specifically I'm interested in the relationship between the invariants of a link and the geometry of its complement.

I received my Bachelors in Math and Physics from Bloomsburg University of Pennsylvania (now Commonwealth University of Pennsylvania) in 2022.

Check out my research and teaching experience and philosophy, as well as some service activities I'm a part of.
